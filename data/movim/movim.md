## Overview

Movim is a modern, federated social network based on XMPP, with a fully featured group-chat, subscriptions and microblogging.

## Features

- Federated social network
- XMPP-based
- Group chat
- Subscriptions
- Microblogging
- Self-hosted deployment
- PHP-based backend
- Docker support

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the AGPL-3.0 license.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via Docker or from source.
