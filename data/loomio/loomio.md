## Overview

Loomio is a collaborative decision-making tool that makes it easy for anyone to participate in decisions which affect them.

## Features

- Collaborative decision-making
- Group discussions
- Decision participation
- Self-hosted deployment
- Docker support

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the AGPL-3.0 license.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via Docker or from source.
