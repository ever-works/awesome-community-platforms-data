## Overview

diaspora* is a distributed social networking server that enables users to run their own pod and connect with others across the federated network.

## Features

- Distributed social networking
- Federated network
- User-owned pods
- Self-hosted deployment
- Ruby-based backend

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the AGPL-3.0 license.

## Deployment

Self-hosted from source.
