## Overview

Enigma 1/2 BBS is a modern, multi-platform BBS engine supporting unlimited callers and legacy DOS door games.

## Features

- Multi-platform BBS engine
- Unlimited callers
- Legacy DOS door game support
- Self-hosted deployment

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the BSD-2-Clause license.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via Docker or from source.
