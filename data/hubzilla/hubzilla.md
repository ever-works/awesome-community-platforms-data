## Overview

Hubzilla is a decentralized platform for identity, privacy, publishing, sharing, cloud storage, and communications/social networking.

## Features

- Decentralized identity
- Privacy controls
- Publishing tools
- Cloud storage
- Communications and social networking
- Self-hosted deployment
- PHP-based backend

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the MIT license.

## Deployment

Self-hosted from source.
