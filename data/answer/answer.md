## Overview

Answer is knowledge-based community software designed for building Q&A communities for product technical support, customer support, and user communication.

## Features

- Q&A community platform
- Technical support forums
- Customer support communication
- Self-hosted deployment
- Go-based backend
- Docker support

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the Apache-2.0 license.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via Docker or from source.
