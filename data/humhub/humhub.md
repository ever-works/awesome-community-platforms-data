## Overview

HumHub is a flexible kit for private social networks, enabling organizations to build social intranets and community platforms.

## Features

- Private social network builder
- Social intranet platform
- Community platform
- Self-hosted deployment
- PHP-based backend

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the AGPL-3.0 license.

## Deployment

Self-hosted from source.
