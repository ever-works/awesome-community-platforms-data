## Overview

Pleroma is a federated microblogging server compatible with Mastodon, GNU social, and ActivityPub.

## Features

- Federated microblogging
- ActivityPub protocol
- Mastodon compatibility
- GNU social compatibility
- Self-hosted deployment
- Elixir-based backend

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the AGPL-3.0 license.

## Deployment

Self-hosted from source.
