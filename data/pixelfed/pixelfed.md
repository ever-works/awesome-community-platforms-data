## Overview

PixelFed is an ethical photo sharing platform powered by ActivityPub federation, serving as an alternative to Instagram.

## Features

- Photo sharing platform
- ActivityPub federation
- Instagram alternative
- Self-hosted deployment
- PHP-based backend

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the AGPL-3.0 license.

## Deployment

Self-hosted from source.
