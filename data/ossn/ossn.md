## Overview

OSSN is social networking software that allows you to make a social networking website and helps members build social relationships.

## Features

- Social networking website builder
- Member relationship building
- Self-hosted deployment
- PHP-based backend

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the CAL-1.0 license.

## Deployment

Self-hosted from source.
