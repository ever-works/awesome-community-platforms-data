## Overview

Talkyard is a community platform where users can suggest ideas and get questions answered, with friendly open-ended discussions and chat. It combines features of Slack, StackOverflow, Discourse, Reddit, and Disqus.

## Features

- Community platform
- Idea suggestions
- Q&A discussions
- Open-ended discussions
- Chat
- Self-hosted deployment
- Scala-based backend
- Docker support

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the AGPL-3.0 license.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via Docker or from source.
